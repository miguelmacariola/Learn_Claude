DepEd ICTS AI Platform

📘 User Guide & Help

Welcome to the DepEd ICTS AI Prompt Engineering Courseware. This tool is designed to help you master AI communication safely and effectively.

How to use this platform:

  • Navigation: Use the "Next" and "Back" buttons at the bottom. The sidebar shows your overall progress.
  • Sequential Learning: You must read the current page or complete the current quiz before you can proceed to the next module. Locked modules have a padlock icon.
  • Auto-Save: Your progress is saved automatically. If you close this window and return later, you will resume exactly where you left off.
  • Role Specificity: The examples provided in the later modules will adapt based on the role you select during Onboarding.
  • Offline Capability: This application runs entirely on your local machine. No sensitive data is transmitted externally.
Need technical support? Contact the System Administrator at ICTS.
